1

You can reserve your seat with either a 30% advance or full payment. Scan our official Kotak Bank UPI QR code or transfer directly to our Kotak Mahindra Bank account. Once you transfer, submit your 12-digit UTR reference. Our team verifies it and sends your confirmed ticket summary on WhatsApp.

We believe in polite, fair policies. Cancellations made 7 or more days before trip departure receive a 100% refund (minus train ticket cancellation charges if already booked). Cancellations 3 to 6 days prior receive a 50% refund. Please check our detailed Refund Policy page for full terms.
