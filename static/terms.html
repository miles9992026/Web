Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to HappyMiles Tours. By booking a trip, submitting a booking form, or participating in our group tours, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms.

1. Bookings and Payments

All bookings require either a 30% advance deposit or full payment via our designated Kotak Mahindra Bank account or official UPI ID. Seats are confirmed only after manual verification of your 12-digit UTR reference. The remaining balance (if any) must be settled upon boarding or as instructed by your Trip Captain.

2. Traveler Responsibilities

Travelers must carry valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhar / Passport / Voter ID) during the trip for hotel check-ins and train travel. Travelers are responsible for their personal belongings, luggage, and respectful conduct towards co-travelers and local communities.

3. Itinerary Adjustments & Unforeseen Events

While we make every effort to adhere strictly to published itineraries, changes may occur due to weather anomalies, landslides, road closures, train delays, or government regulations. In such circumstances, our priority remains the safety of all travelers, and substitute arrangements will be coordinated by our Trip Captain.

4. Code of Conduct

HappyMiles Tours maintains a zero-tolerance policy against harassment, disruptive behavior, or illegal substance usage. We foster a warm, welcoming, family-friendly and solo-friendly atmosphere on every journey.
