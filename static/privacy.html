Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy explains how HappyMiles Tours collects, uses, and safeguards information provided by visitors and travelers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential booking details including your name, WhatsApp phone number, optional email address, pickup preferences, and payment transaction reference (UTR) for verifying bookings and coordinating your trip.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your details are used exclusively to process your reservation, coordinate Hyderabad pickup schedules, add you to your batch's coordination group, and assist you before and during the trip. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or spam advertising lists.

3. Photographs & Consent

Group photographs taken during our trips are shared with our travelers' explicit consent on our website and social channels. If you ever prefer a specific image of yourself removed, simply message us on WhatsApp or email happymilestravelss@gmail.com and we will promptly take it down.

4. Security

All sensitive metadata (EXIF/GPS) is stripped from publicly displayed images, and data submitted through our booking system is encrypted in transit.
