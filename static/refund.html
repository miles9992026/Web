Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent cancellation guidelines

At HappyMiles Tours, we understand that life happens and plans can change. Our cancellation rules are crafted to be as fair and polite as possible while covering committed vendor costs (train reservations, private tempo hires, resort blockouts).

Cancellation Slabs (Before Departure Date)

Timing of Notice Refund Amount
7 or more days prior to trip start 100% Refund (minus non-refundable train tickets)
3 to 6 days prior to trip start 50% Refund
Within 48 hours or No-Show No refund (seat transfer to a friend allowed)

Seat Transfer Option

If you cannot attend, you may transfer your seat to a friend or family member free of cost, up to 12 hours before departure (subject to train ticket name change feasibility or road trip transport).

How to Request a Refund

Please message us on WhatsApp at +91 8074016910 or email happymilestravelss@gmail.com with your Booking Reference number (e.g. HM-202611-XXXX). Refunds are processed back to the original UPI/Bank account within 3 to 5 business days.
